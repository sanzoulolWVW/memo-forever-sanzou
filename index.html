05Graphics settingsSharper scenery & more detail.

Copy a command, paste it into the game chat and press Enter. Apply them one at a time to compare the result.

  • Sharpness

    Enables sharpening even without FSR upscaling.

    /console ResampleAlwaysSharpen 1
  • Vegetation

    Increases the density of grass, flowers and small ground details.

    /console groundEffectDensity 256
  • View distance

    Requests a longer landscape view distance, within the client’s limits.

    /console farclip 7000
  • Small details

    Lowers the small-object culling threshold to retain more distant details.

    /console lodObjectCullSize 8
  • Scenery detail

    Pushes back transitions to simpler versions of scenery objects.

    /console lodObjectFadeScale 200

More detail may reduce FPS. On Forever, some values may be capped or ignored by the client. Note your current settings before changing them.
